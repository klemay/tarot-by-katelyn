Based in Brooklyn, NY and available for online readings as well as events, cafes, bars, markets, and festivals.

With over 10 years of experience, I help people use tarot for creativity, personal development, self-reflection, and fun. My readings will help you see your situation clearly, explore your options, and form a plan of action.

In addition to reading tarot professionally, I co-created Tarot Bot and have spent years writing educational resources to help people deepen their understanding of tarot.

I work to create an affirming space for people of all identities. I want you to feel like you can bring your whole self to a reading and be honored and respected for who you are.

My approach to tarot is to treat the practice like a mirror, not a crystal ball— rather than trying to predict the future, I use tarot to help uncover truths you may already know but haven't yet seen clearly.

If you'd like to work together 1:1, you can book a reading here. Or, you can send me an email to inquire about booking me for an event.

  • Tarot readings are for entertainment purposes only.
  • I will never use AI for any part of your tarot reading, nor will I feed your reading or data into an LLM.
  • I do not do readings for health or legal related matters.
  • We can explore mental health topics together, but not as a replacement for support from a mental healthcare professional.
  • While you can ask questions that involve a third party, I can help you phrase your question in terms of how you are contributing to the situation.
  • Repeat readings on the same question are subject to my discretion. If I sense an unhealthy pattern is forming, I will decline a repeat reading and refund you in full.
